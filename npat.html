📖 Rules & Scoring

  • Random 1 & 2 Letter Starts: Rounds can draw single letters (e.g. A, M, S) or 2-letter combos (e.g. AN, CH, TH, SH, PR).
  • Category-Specific Online Check: Animals are verified against creatures/species, Places against geography, Movies against cinema/films, and Things against physical objects/items.
  • One-by-One Voting: Any doubtful word is voted on sequentially before scores are shown. Only a strict majority (Yes > No) passes.
  • Admin Spell Check: After voting, the Host can adjust spellings (e.g., keeping udumb and urumb distinct or merging typos) and override validity.
  • Tiered Matching Points:
    • 10 pts — Unique valid word
    • 5 pts — Matched by 2 players
    • 4 pts — Matched by 3 players
    • 3 pts — Matched by 4 players (decreases down to 1 pt)
    • 0 pts — Empty or Invalid
