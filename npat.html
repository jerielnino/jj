📖 How to Play

Name Place Animal Thing (Film Edition) is a fast-paced multiplayer word race over local Wi-Fi / LAN!

  • A random letter is drawn at the start of each round.
  • Progress step-by-step through the 5 categories: Name ➔ Place ➔ Animal ➔ Thing ➔ Film, entering an item for each and clicking Next until you click Submit.
  • Scoring Rules:
    • 10 Points for each unique guess that no one else wrote.
    • 5 Points for answers that match another player's guess.
    • Spelling similarity is automatically matched (e.g., "Deli" and "Delhi" are treated as identical and therefore not unique).
    • 0 Points if empty or begins with the wrong letter.
  • Online verification automatically looks up photos and definitions from Wikipedia. If offline, the game plays smoothly with local dictionary checks!
  • The player with the highest total score at the end wins!
