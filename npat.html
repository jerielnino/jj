📖 Rules & Scoring

  • Random 1 & 2 Letter Starts: Rounds can draw single letters (e.g. A, M, S) or 2-letter combos (e.g. AN, CH, TH, SH, PR).
  • Category-Specific Online Check: Animals are verified against creatures/species, Places against geography, Movies against cinema/films, and Things against physical objects/items.
  • One-by-One Voting & Full Transparency: Any doubtful word is voted on sequentially by all players before scores are shown. The final scoreboard displays the complete voting breakdown showing who voted For (👍) and Against (👎) each word.
  • Smart Close-Call Check Only: Host never has to validate every word! Only if two players write close spellings (like Rishi / Rashi) does the Host choose Valid 10 pts each or Same Matched 5 pts each.
  • Points System:
    • 10 pts — Unique valid word (or close call marked as distinct valid words)
    • 5 pts — Same / Matched valid word (for each matching player)
    • 0 pts — Empty or Invalid
