⏱️ The 5-Hour Lock Rule

  • Lock Deadline: Kickoff - 5 Hours
  • Opponent Lineups: Hidden before lock
  • Live Reveal: Revealed upon lock
  • Substitutions: Locked after 5h

⚽ Goal & Assist Scoring

  • Forward Goal: +4 pts
  • Midfielder Goal: +5 pts
  • Defender / GK Goal: +6 pts
  • Any Assist: +3 pts
  • Captain Multiplier: 2x Total

🛡️ Defense & Goalkeeping

  • Clean Sheet (DEF/GK, 60+m): +4 pts
  • Clean Sheet (MID, 60+m): +1 pt
  • Every 3 Saves (GK): +1 pt
  • Penalty Save: +5 pts
  • Every 2 Goals Conceded: -1 pt

⚠️ Discipline & Deductions

  • Playing 1 - 59 mins: +1 pt
  • Playing 60+ mins: +2 pts
  • Yellow Card: -1 pt
  • Red Card: -3 pts
  • Penalty Miss / Own Goal: -2 pts
  • Bonus Points (BPS): +1 to +3 pts

👥 15-Player Squad & Bench Rules

  • Full Squad: 15 Players (11 Starting + 4 Bench)
  • First XI Split: Strict 6:5 Split (Max 6 from one club)
  • Bench Split: Strict 2:2 Split (2 from each club)
  • Bench Breakdown: 1 GK Sub + 3 Outfield Subs
  • Max Budget: £100.0M for all 15 Players
  • Auto-Substitution: Priority Sub 1 → 2 → 3 if 0 mins
  • Captain Multiplier: 2x Match Points
  • Vice-Captain: Backup 2x if Captain 0 mins
