📖 How to Play Number Duel

The Objective: Crack your opponent's secret code before they crack yours!

  • Secret Selection: Each player secretly picks a 3, 4, 5, or 6-digit number. You can choose whether digits must be unique or if duplicates like 9954 are allowed.
  • Taking Turns: Players take turns guessing the other player's secret.
  • Feedback Clues:
    • Total Correct: How many digits in your guess exist in the secret code.
    • Order Correct: How many digits are in the exact same slot position!
    • Example: If secret is 4678 and guess is 6490 ➔ returns 2 correct, 0 order correct (because 4 and 6 exist in the code, but neither is in the right position).
  • Deduction Scratchpad & Per-Digit Notes: Tap digits 0-9 on the Scratchpad, inside Slot Notes, or directly on any digit in your Guess History to cycle through Eliminated (Red ❌), Candidate/Guess (Orange ❓), Confirmed (Green 🎯), and Clear.
  • Tournaments:
    • 2 Players: Head-to-Head 1v1 showdown.
    • 3 Players: Triangle Clash (Round-robin duels where each pair plays; top 2 advance to the Grand Final!).
    • 4 Players: Knockout Tournament (Semifinals ➔ Grand Final & 3rd place match).
  • Victory: The first player to get all digits in exact order (e.g. 4 order correct for a 4-digit code) wins!
